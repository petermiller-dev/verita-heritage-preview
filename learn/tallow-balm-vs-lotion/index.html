Tallow balm vs. lotion

Texture and spread

A balm like ours has an oil-and-fat base and softens with the warmth of your hands. Many lotions contain water as part of an emulsion and can be easier to spread over a larger area. Thickness and finish vary by formula.

Where each fits

A dab of balm is handy for knuckles and elbows. A pump lotion can be convenient when you want to quickly cover your arms or legs. Think about where you use moisturizer most and how you want it to feel afterward.

What the research can tell you

A 2024 scoping review on tallow and skin found gaps in the evidence. Claims about treating skin conditions go beyond what we can say about our balm. We describe it by its ingredients, texture, and how we use it.
