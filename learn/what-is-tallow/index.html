What is beef tallow?

From beef fat to balm

Beef tallow is rendered beef fat. Rendering uses heat to separate fat from the surrounding tissue. The fat is then strained and allowed to cool. Tallow can be firm at room temperature and softens with warmth.

Why we use it

We make a concentrated balm that softens between your fingertips. In our Rose Scent balm, we pair tallow with jojoba oil to make it easier to spread a small amount where you want it. The finished texture depends on the whole recipe.

Reading the label

When comparing jars, look for the full ingredient list, scent ingredients, and directions. A grass-fed description tells you about sourcing. The ingredient panel tells you what else is in the jar.
