Easy as Pie Simmer Pot

Apple pie in the air

Dried apple slices and warm spices bring the scent of an apple pie to the kitchen. Put Easy as Pie on the stove while you wrap gifts, set the table, or get the house ready for company.

A little gift for the kitchen

Tuck a pouch into a hostess gift or pair it with a handwritten note. It’s a simple way to share the scent of a slow afternoon at home.

How to use

Follow the directions on the pouch. Add the blend to a pot of water and let it simmer gently, adding water as needed. Keep the pot attended and take it off the heat before the water runs low. For home fragrance only.
