Magnesium Mist

A simple mist for the end of the day

Two ingredients go into our Magnesium Mist: purified water and magnesium chloride. We bottle it in small batches for a simple step after a shower or as you get ready for bed.

How to use

Mist lightly onto arms, legs, or torso. The mineral salts can feel tingly, especially on freshly shaved skin. Follow the directions on the bottle.

Size: 2 oz / 60 mL
