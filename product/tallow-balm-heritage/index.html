Rose Scent Tallow Skin Balm

A little balm, a soft rose scent

Our Rose Scent Tallow Skin Balm brings a soft floral note to a familiar daily ritual. Warm a little between your fingertips and smooth it onto dry hands, elbows, or wherever your skin could use extra moisture. The balm is rich enough that you can start with a pea-sized amount and add more as needed.

Made in small batches in Texas

Our family makes this balm with grass-fed, grass-finished tallow and jojoba oil. It grew out of years of refining the skincare we wanted to use in our own home. Rose gives this version a floral finish.
