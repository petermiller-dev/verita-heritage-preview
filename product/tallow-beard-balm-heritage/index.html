Tallow Beard Balm

Softens coarse hair and gives it a little shape

We make this beard balm in small batches with grass-fed tallow, mango butter, jojoba, squalane, and beeswax. Work a little through your beard for a softer feel and light hold.

Choose your scent

  • Original pairs ho wood and black spruce with petitgrain and palmarosa.
  • Balsam Woods has a warm, slightly smoky scent with a touch of sweetness from Balsam of Peru.

How to use

Warm a little between your palms, work it through your beard, and shape with your fingers or a comb.

Size: 2 oz / 56 g
